1. Core DefinitionWhat it is: A practical, reflective examination consisting of the development of a "product" (software, design, business plan, etc.) and digital documentation.Platform: Conducted and submitted exclusively via PebblePad.Critical Rule: You must follow the three phases in the exact order specified. Failure to adhere to the sequence results in a failed exam.2. The Three PhasesEach phase requires submission and typically involves waiting for tutor feedback (approx. 7 days) before moving to the next.Phase 1: ConceptionGoal: Define the "What, Why, and How."Output: * Text (max. 0.5 DIN A4 page) explaining the concept, motivation, and methodology.Initial sketches, drafts, or diagrams.Key Questions: What is the aim? What are the pros/cons of the ideas? Which tools/methods were chosen?Phase 2: Development & ReflectionGoal: Implement the project and monitor progress.Output: * Text (max. 0.5 DIN A4 page) explaining the implementation process.Digital designs or intermediate steps (prototypes).Focus: Identifying risks, managing resources (time/tech), and tracking status.Phase 3: FinalizationGoal: Submit the final product and a holistic reflection.Output:2-Page Abstract (PDF): Reflection on goals vs. results, methodology, and personal learning.Final Product: The completed work.Resubmission: You must re-upload the (potentially revised) results from Phase 1 and Phase 2.Optional: ZIP folder with additional resources.3.
